Week 0 – A Good Start

Focus: Self-Awareness, Creative Thinking & Personal Development

Week 0 marked the beginning of our Protosem learning journey. The week was designed to help us start the program with a positive mindset while developing self-awareness and reflective thinking.

One of the activities involved reading comics from Zen Pencils, which encouraged us to look at different ideas, experiences, and perspectives in a creative and meaningful way. This activity helped us understand how simple stories and illustrations can communicate deeper thoughts and life lessons.

We also took the 16 Personality Test, which provided insights into our personality traits, strengths, behavioral patterns, and areas for self-reflection. The activity helped us better understand ourselves and recognize how our personality can influence the way we learn, communicate, and work with others.

Key Learnings

  • Developing greater self-awareness and understanding of personal traits.
  • Improving creative and reflective thinking through comic-based learning.
  • Understanding personal strengths and areas for improvement.
  • Recognizing the importance of a positive mindset at the beginning of a learning journey.
  • Understanding how personality can influence communication, teamwork, and learning.

Outcome

By the end of Week 0, I gained a better understanding of my personality, strengths, and areas for self-improvement. The activities provided a meaningful starting point for the Protosem journey and encouraged me to approach the upcoming learning experiences with greater self-awareness, curiosity, and a positive mindset.
